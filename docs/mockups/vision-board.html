4 PLAYER CO-OP
HEIST CREW

Why it's built this way

  • One shape, readable at 64px. The mask silhouette survives being shrunk to a phone thumbnail. Detailed art turns to mush at that size, which is where most indie Roblox icons die.
  • Gold on near-black. Roblox's browse grid is a wall of bright saturated cartoon icons. Going dark and expensive-looking is how you stand out in a row of them, and it matches the lobby palette already in HeistBuilder.
  • The red "4 PLAYER CO-OP" flag. Co-op is the actual differentiator, so it goes on the icon, not in the description nobody reads.
  • Heavy italic title with a hard drop shadow. Chunky bold type is the Roblox house style. This borrows the readability without the childish look.
  • Thumbnails are a separate job. The icon is the square. The wide thumbnails are where you show four characters mid-escape with the vault open. Those get made from real in-game screenshots once the build is worth screenshotting.
